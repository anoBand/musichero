# Instrument samples

The MP3 files in this folder are single notes rendered from the **FluidR3_GM** SoundFont by Frank Wen,
taken from [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), where FluidR3_GM is
distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Only the notes the app needs are included (one every three semitones). The files are otherwise unmodified.
